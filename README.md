# Drum scores

Drum parts transcribed automatically from recordings with drum2score (drum separation, onset detection,
tempo and meter tracking, standard drum-set notation). Each page shows the score, a step grid, and plays the
notation back on a recorded acoustic drum kit, in sync with the song playing from YouTube's embedded player. No song
recordings are stored here.

These are unofficial, automatic fan transcriptions for practice and study; they will contain mistakes.
All songs belong to their respective rights holders.
